# Code of Conduct

Payeebook follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient. Many people who use and improve Payeebook are
caring for a parent, child or sibling and are new to both accounting and open
source. Assume good intent, explain rather than correct, and keep discussions
about the work.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, insults, publishing anyone's
private information (including financial or case details), and sustained
disruption. Maintainers may remove comments or block participants who do this.

Report concerns privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/lobojoel2003). All reports are handled
confidentially.
